Privacy Policy for Vidzo

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Vidzo ("the App", "we", "us") is a web browser app for Android published by ITCODE INFOTECH. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

Summary

Vidzo does not require account creation or sign-in. Your browsing history, bookmarks, tab state, and app settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us or to any server we control. The only data leaving your device is the advertising-related data described below, which is collected by our ad provider, Google AdMob, to show ads in the App.

Data stored on your device only

The following is stored locally on your device (via Android DataStore) and never leaves it:

  • Browsing history (most recent 300 entries)
  • Open tabs and their state
  • Shortcuts you add to the home screen
  • App settings: theme, default search engine, default browser status
  • Your 4-digit app lock PIN — stored only as a salted SHA-256 hash, never in plain text, and never leaves your device

Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of this data.

Data collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

Vidzo shows ads using Google AdMob (app open ads, native ads, and optional rewarded video ads). AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • General device and app information (device model, OS version, app version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address), where permitted
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and their advertising data practices. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Install source / referrer data

If you installed Vidzo via a marketing link, the Google Play Install Referrer API provides the App with a short campaign code (e.g. which promotional link you used). This code only determines which shortcut/campaign page is shown on your home screen and is not sent to any server we control.

Permissions the App uses

  • Internet access — required to browse the web and load ads
  • Storage (downloads) — to save files you choose to download through the browser

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions for its own use. Websites you visit may request camera/microphone/location access through the browser; these requests are shown to you directly and are controlled by your own choice, not by us.

Data we do not collect

  • No account registration, no email, no phone number
  • No browsing history, bookmarks, or form data is uploaded to any server we operate
  • We do not sell your data to third parties

Children's privacy

Vidzo is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
