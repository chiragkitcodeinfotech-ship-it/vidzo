Vidzo is a fast, lightweight web browser for Android, built for people who just want to get online quickly without clutter.

FEATURES
• Quick-access home screen with your favorite shortcuts — add or remove sites with a long press
• Multiple tabs that stay open even after you close the app
• Browsing history so you can find pages you visited before
• Search with your choice of 5 search engines, with live suggestions as you type
• Light, dark, or system theme
• Optional 4-digit PIN lock to keep your browser private
• Set Vidzo as your default browser in one tap
• Desktop mode for sites that need the full desktop layout
• Downloads, file uploads, and full-screen video support

PRIVACY
Vidzo doesn't require an account or sign-in. Your history, bookmarks, and settings are stored only on your device — never uploaded to any server we run. Read our full privacy policy for details on how advertising works in the app.

Whether you're quickly checking something online or browsing for a while, Vidzo keeps it simple and fast.
